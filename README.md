🚀 The Beginner Sovereign Stack

New to privacy? Start here. These five tools form the foundation of a secure, private digital life.

1.  **🦁 Brave Browser** (*Easiest*)
    *   **Replace Chrome:** A direct drop-in replacement built on Chromium. It blocks ads and trackers by default, requires no configuration, and is 3x faster than Chrome.
    *   *Action:* Download, import bookmarks, and you're done.

2.  **🔐 Bitwarden** (*Easy*)
    *   **Secure Your Passwords:** Generate unique, random passwords for every site. Store them with end-to-end encryption across all devices.
    *   *Action:* Install the extension, import existing passwords, and start replacing weak ones.

3.  **✉️ Proton Mail** (*Medium*)
    *   **Private Email:** Encrypt your messages so even Proton cannot read them. Migrate gradually from Gmail to protect your communications.
    *   *Action:* Create a free account and forward critical emails.

4.  **🎭 Mullvad VPN** (*Easy*)
    *   **Anonymous Traffic:** Encrypt your ISP's view of your browsing. Accepts cash and Monero, requires no email, and keeps no logs.
    *   *Action:* Download the app, click "Connect," and your ISP goes blind.

5.  **🚪uBlock Origin** (*Easy → Advanced*)
    *   **Kill Ads Everywhere:** A lightweight browser extension that blocks ads, trackers, and malware.
    *   *Advanced:* Combine with **Pi-hole** to block ads on your entire network (TV, console, smart devices) without installing anything on those devices.
